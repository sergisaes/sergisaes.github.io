Research

Paper submitted to IEEE

Real-Time Hybrid Predictive Fuzzy Control for UAV Landing on Moving Platforms

Marcel Naderer, Sergi Sanmartin, Gaëtan Soudée, Yu-Chen Liu, Min-Fan Ricky Lee
MCI Innsbruck · UPC Barcelona · ECE Paris · NTUST Taipei — submitted to IEEE

A real-time landing supervisor for multirotor UAVs on moving decks that fuses a physics-based deck-motion model, a GRU residual predictor with a confidence measure, and a Mamdani fuzzy layer producing interpretable behavioural modes — descending only when confidence and clearance are sufficient, even under wind gusts, irregular waves and vision dropouts.
